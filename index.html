SpecCheck Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

SpecCheck reads hardware and system information from the phone it is installed on and shows it to you. That is all it does.

We collect nothing

There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier, and no server operated by us. The app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

You do not have to take our word for it: the app's About screen counts the permissions declared by the copy installed on your phone and explains each one. At the time of writing there are three, and none of them touches the network.

Files you create stay yours

When you save a spec sheet (CSV) or a snapshot file, it is written to the location you choose with your own system file picker. If you send that file to someone, it travels only where you send it. It never passes through us.

The floating readout

The optional floating readout draws a small panel on top of other apps and needs the "display over other apps" permission plus a foreground service to stay visible. It reads the same values as the main screen and keeps none of them: nothing is stored, logged, or transmitted.

Purchases

Any purchase is handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, this policy and the Google Play Data safety form will both be updated before that version ships.
